IT Enterprise Monitoring & Process Automation

Welcome to the watch floor.

This is your onboarding console for the IT Enterprise Monitoring team at NBK Egypt — everything you need to get ATM, network and banking‑service monitoring access sorted, in one place.

How the team works

The day-to-day work is divided into two connected tracks: Infrastructure and ATM Operations.

Two connected workstreams

Both tracks share the same goal: identify issues early, communicate clearly and follow them through to resolution.

01 · Infrastructure

Monitor the IT estate

Focus on the health of the IT environment and the response to service-impacting issues.

  • Monitor servers, databases and the wider monitoring environment.
  • Send outage notifications for IT-related service interruptions.
  • Open an incident when an issue occurs.
  • Escalate and follow up on issues until they are resolved.
02 · ATM Operations

Follow ATM faults

Monitor ATM faults and service status, then follow reported issues through to resolution.
